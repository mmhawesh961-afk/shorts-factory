Shorts Factory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

What the app does

Shorts Factory is a personal tool that uploads videos to its owner's YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Google data it uses

The app asks for one permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the signed-in account's channel. It does not read, download, or change any other YouTube or Google account data.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's, and it is never sold or shared.

Sharing

No user data is shared with third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
